ABOUT QUANTVESTING

Understand before you act

Quantvesting is a rules-based portfolio intelligence framework designed to help investors understand what they own, what deserves review, and where further research may be worthwhile.

What the Portfolio Assessment does

Upload your current holdings and Quantvesting evaluates them against the configured framework and the latest validated market data available for the assessment. You get a single view across portfolio health, allocation, concentration, holding-level review areas, the current opportunity universe and your portfolio journey.

What to expect

My Portfolio

See portfolio value, deployed value, target value, health, allocation, concentration and holding-level details.

Review Areas

See holdings or situations the framework flags for closer examination, with evidence to help you investigate further.

Opportunity Universe

Explore securities currently ranking highly within the Quantvesting research universe. This is a research view, not a recommendation.

My Journey

Follow the portfolio trajectory across available assessment dates and portfolio-health measures.

HOW TO PREPARE & UPLOAD

Format your current holdings

Use one row for each holding

The simplest CSV uses these three columns:

SymbolSharesAvgCost
RELIANCE202450
TCS103200

Common broker/export names such as Ticker, Quantity and Average Price are accepted. If you have multiple account rows for the same symbol, Quantvesting combines them into one holding using weighted-average cost.

Download sample myPortfolioStocks.csv

Upload the CSV

Save your current holdings as a CSV, return to Assess My Portfolio, choose the file, and select Assess My Portfolio. You do not need to prepare any code or special software.

Use current holdings rather than a transaction history. Quantvesting assesses the holdings represented by the uploaded file.

HOW TO READ

Understand your assessment report

My Portfolio

This tab gives the overall picture: portfolio value, deployed value, target value, Portfolio Health, allocation, concentration and the current holding-level view.

Review Areas

This tab shows holdings or situations the configured framework has flagged for a closer look, including capital-rotation review. A review label is a prompt to examine the evidence, not a buy or sell instruction.

Opportunity Universe

This shows the top 40 ranked Quantvesting securities, then keeps only those with Upside greater than 20%. It is not a list of investment recommendations.

My Journey

This shows the portfolio trajectory across available assessment dates, including value, Portfolio Health and the Core, Legacy and Outside-universe mix.

Key terms and labels

Portfolio Health

Share of portfolio value currently meeting the framework's health conditions. It is separate from portfolio allocation.

Current value

The current value represented by the assessment's available market data.

Deployed value

The portfolio value basis represented by the uploaded holdings and their average costs.

Target value / Target profit

The portfolio-level value and profit implied by the configured holding targets where targets are available.

Core / Legacy / Outside universe

Classification labels describing where a holding sits within the current Quantvesting framework.

Review candidate

The framework has identified the security for review based on its configured ranking or evidence.

Target reached — review

The current price has reached or exceeded the configured FTT reference level. Review the underlying information before making a decision.

Legacy holding — review

An existing legacy holding for which the framework has raised a review condition.

Rotation review

The framework has reached a configured thesis-capture threshold and is flagging a comparison for review.

Strong rotation review

A stronger form of the configured rotation-review flag, indicating a higher-priority comparison within the framework.

No current review

No configured review threshold is currently met for this holding.

Recent market data

The assessment is using a market snapshot considered recent by the framework. The displayed IST timestamp tells you when that snapshot was updated.

Quantvesting labels describe a rules-based assessment. They are not investment advice or a recommendation to buy, sell or hold any security.
